## 🌷 A little about me

Hello! I'm Ellie, a Biochemistry graduate, Research Assistant and an enthusiastic explorer of the computational sciences.

I'm fascinated by the intersection of biology, technology and data. My journey has taken me from laboratory microbiology into bacterial genomics, microbiome science and bioinformatics.

I enjoy asking questions, experimenting with unfamiliar tools and figuring things out along the way.

I don't believe I need to know everything before I start building something. Sometimes the most interesting discoveries begin with simply being curious enough to try.

**A few things that describe me:**

- 🧬 Interested in microbiomes, bacterial genomics and infectious diseases.
- 🔬 Working at the intersection of laboratory research and computational biology.
- 💻 Exploring programming, data analysis and bioinformatics.
- 🌱 Always learning something new.
- 💡 Excited by ideas that connect science with real-world problems.
