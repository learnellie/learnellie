## A little about me

Hello! I'm Bernice, but I go by Ellie.

I'm a Biochemistry graduate and Research Assistant with an interest in understanding biological systems through both laboratory research and computational approaches.

My interests have gradually expanded into microbiome science, bacterial genomics, metagenomics and bioinformatics.

I'm particularly fascinated by the questions that emerge when biology meets data. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar tools, experimenting with ideas and learning through practical projects.

I'm still discovering what is possible, and that's the exciting part.

## What I'm curious about

* **Microbiome science:** Understanding microbial communities and host-microbe interactions.
* **Bacterial genomics:** Exploring bacterial diversity, evolution and genomic variation.
* **Metagenomics:** Investigating microbial communities through sequencing data.
* **Infectious diseases:** Understanding microbial processes and their implications for health.
* **Computational biology:** Applying programming, statistics and mathematical models to biological questions.
